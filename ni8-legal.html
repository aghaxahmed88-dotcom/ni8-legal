Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Ni8 — Health & Fitness is built to work fully offline. This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and how it is stored.

Data we store

All your data — step counts, habits, workout log, timetable, streaks, alarms and settings — is stored only in the app's private storage on your device. Nothing is uploaded to a server, and Ni8 does not require an account or sign-in.

Permissions we request

  • Physical activity / motion sensors — required to count your steps and show your daily step goal and history. Without it, step tracking won't work.
  • Notifications — required to ring alarms and habit/timetable reminders, even when the app is closed. You can turn this off any time from Settings → Notifications.
  • Health Connect (optional) — if you connect it, step data is synced from Health Connect so your totals stay accurate across apps. This stays on your device like everything else.

What Ni8 does not do

  • No ads, no ad trackers, no analytics SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of your data with third parties.
  • No internet connection is required for the app to work.

Your control over your data

  • Backup — save all your data to a file you control, from Settings.
  • Restore — load a previous backup file on this or a new device.
  • Reset — permanently erase all app data from this device, from Settings.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update date above will change. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
